La Habra's football team is one of four left in CIF Southern Section Division 4. The Highlanders play Quartz Hill in the semifinals this Friday, Nov. 20, at Quartz Hill High in the Antelope Valley.

Kickoff is at 7 pm.

**How La Habra got here**

La Habra went 0-5 in league play and finished the regular season 5-5. Then the playoffs started.

In the first round on Thursday, Nov. 5, the Highlanders beat Etiwanda 42-35 at home. Last Friday, Nov. 13, they hosted Cathedral and won 41-35 on a play as time ran out. That makes them 7-5, with two straight one-score playoff wins.

**The other side**

Quartz Hill is 11-1. The Royals beat Hart 35-14 at home in the first round, then got past King 14-11 in the quarterfinals. MaxPreps says the King game was their tenth straight win. Their last two games were low-scoring, while La Habra has put up more than 40 points in both of its playoff games.

**What's at stake**

The winner plays for the Division 4 title against the winner of the other semifinal, Chino Hills (8-4) at Downey (8-4).

**The rest of the local teams**

La Habra is the last local football team still playing. Sonora's season ended in the first round, and Whittier Christian's ended in the Division 13 quarterfinals.
