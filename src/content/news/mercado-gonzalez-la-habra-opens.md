If you shop at the Northgate González Market at 1305 W. Whittier Blvd., it looks different now. The store has been turned into a Mercado González, the company's food hall format, and it opened with a ceremony on the store's patio in late October.

La Habra is only the second Mercado González. The first opened in Costa Mesa in 2023. Northgate, an Anaheim company founded in 1980, picked the La Habra store because it's one of its longest-running and best-selling neighborhood locations.

**What's inside**

It's still a full grocery store, with produce (including an organic section), meat, and pantry staples. What's new is everything around it:

- Food stalls: Taquería Los Mesquites, Carnitas Don Miguel, Guisados Las Cazuelas, Rosticería Los 13 Pollos and Cevichería El Pariente.
- In-house counters for tamales, paletas, pastries, tortillas and meat.
- El Moro Churrería, the Mexico City churro and hot chocolate shop.
- Entre Nos Bar, with Mexican beers, cocktails, mezcal and tequila.
- A gifts and sweets section with crafts and traditional candy.

There's also a mural above the deli case by local artist Kris Aguilar. It shows the town in Mexico where the González family lived before they came to the U.S.

**Hours**

6 am to 10 pm Sunday through Thursday, and 6 am to 11 pm Friday and Saturday.

**For the neighborhood**

The company says more than 340 people work at the La Habra store. At the opening, Northgate gave more than $20,000 to local schools and community groups, and it supports the city's Holiday Wishes giving program, which helps more than 350 local families.
