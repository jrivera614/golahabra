La Habra is close to making it illegal to sell nitrous oxide for getting high, the gas sold in small canisters often called whippets.

The City Council held a public hearing and first reading of the ordinance on Oct. 19. It came back for a second reading and adoption on the council's Nov. 2 consent calendar. The ordinance, No. CC 2026-09, adds a new chapter to the city code banning the sale or distribution of nitrous oxide. We'll confirm the final vote once the city posts the official minutes, which usually come out about two weeks after a meeting.

**What it covers**

According to the draft reviewed by Whittier 360 News Network, the ban goes beyond the gas itself. It also covers the things used to dispense or hold it, like cartridges, cylinders, balloons and nozzles. Selling, offering or handing them out would all be off limits.

**What's still allowed**

The draft carves out everyday and professional uses, including:

- Whipped cream and other food that uses the gas as a propellant
- Medical and dental care, and pharmacies
- Wholesale supplies for businesses and products bought for resale
- Flavorless products made for car performance, sold by licensed retailers

**Penalties**

Breaking the law would be a misdemeanor, with a fine of up to $1,000, up to six months in county jail, or both. Each day a violation continues can count as a separate offense. The city could also use civil citations.

If adopted, the ordinance takes effect 30 days after it passes.

**How we got here**

The idea came out of the council's broader look at smoke shops this summer. In July, the council extended its moratorium on new smoke shops and asked staff to come back with options on nitrous oxide and kratom. City staff said state law is hard to enforce because cases can require proof of what a seller knew or what a buyer intended. Staff expects little cost to the city, with enforcement handled through regular inspections and complaints.

Kratom is on a slower track. At the Oct. 19 meeting, the council was asked whether to pause work on a kratom sales ban for now.

The next council meeting is Monday, Nov. 16, at 6:30 pm at City Hall, 100 E. La Habra Blvd.
