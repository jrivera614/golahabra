Mayor José Medrano and Councilmember Jim Gomez are out in front in the race for two La Habra City Council seats, based on the latest unofficial count from the Orange County Registrar of Voters.

**The race**

La Habra's five council members are elected citywide to four-year terms. Two seats were on the Nov. 3 ballot, and voters could pick up to two of four candidates. Here's how they were listed on the ballot:

- José Medrano, Mayor, City of La Habra
- James "Jim" Gomez, Businessman/Councilmember
- Susan Pritchard, Retired Teacher
- Miles C. Matias, IT Analyst/Instructor

**Where the count stands**

As of the registrar's update on Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 4:47 pm:

- Medrano: 10,788 votes (36.4%)
- Gomez: 8,285 votes (28%)
- Pritchard: 6,284 votes (21.2%)
- Matias: 4,264 votes (14.4%)

That's 29,621 votes cast in the race so far. Gomez holds the second seat by about 2,000 votes over Pritchard. Medrano and Gomez were also first and second in the first count on election night.

**Not final yet**

These numbers are unofficial. The registrar posts new totals every weekday at 5 pm until the results are certified.

We'll update this story when the count is final.
