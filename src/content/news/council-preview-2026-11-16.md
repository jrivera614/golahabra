The La Habra City Council meets Monday, Nov. 16, at 6:30 pm in the Council Chamber at City Hall, 100 E. La Habra Blvd. Here's what's on the agenda that could affect you.

**Watering rules for decorative grass**

The council, sitting with the city's Utility Authority, is set to hold a public hearing and first vote on an ordinance about "nonfunctional turf." That's grass that's just for looks, like lawns in parking lots, street medians and outside businesses. Grass people actually use, like park fields and play areas, doesn't count.

A 2023 state law, AB 1572, says drinking water can't be used to water that kind of grass, and cities have to add the rule to their own codes by Jan. 1, 2027. The ban phases in: city and other public properties first in 2027, then commercial, industrial and institutional properties in 2028, then HOA and condo common areas in 2029. Watering to keep trees and other plants healthy, or to deal with a health and safety need, would still be allowed. The draft does not cover single-family homes.

**Zoning code cleanup**

A second public hearing covers a batch of small changes to the city's zoning code, meant to make the rules more consistent and easier for city staff to apply. The Planning Commission recommended it 3-0 on Oct. 12, with two commissioners absent.

**Sidewalks, striping and EV chargers**

The consent calendar, usually approved in one vote, includes:

- Two sidewalk contracts: a sidewalk grinding program (up to $442,981) and sidewalk improvements (up to $585,612)
- Annual street striping work, up to $191,980
- An EV charging station project at the La Habra Community Center, up to $119,850
- A $35,374 contract with Pyro Spectaculars for the 2027 Fourth of July fireworks
- Signing off on finished work for traffic signal timing along Lambert Road and alley water line upgrades

The council will also recognize the La Habra Pop Warner 10U team for its 2026 Orange Bowl championship.

The closed session set for 5:30 pm was canceled. The next meeting is Monday, Dec. 7.
