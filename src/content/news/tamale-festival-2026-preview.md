One of La Habra's biggest days of the year is almost here. The 13th annual Tamale Festival runs Saturday, Dec. 5, from 10 am to 8 pm.

**Where it is**

The festival fills Euclid St. between La Habra Blvd. and Bridenbecker Ave. It also spreads into City Hall and its parking lot, First and Second Avenues, Portola Park and Brio Park.

**What's going on**

There's live entertainment on three stages: the Main Stage at Portola Park, a Community Stage at City Hall, and the stage for the Holiday Tree Lighting Ceremony. The night wraps up with a holiday fireworks show. The festival hasn't posted times for the tree lighting or the fireworks yet, so check the festival site closer to the day.

**For kids**

There are three kid areas:
- **City Hall Kid Zone**, 10 am to 6 pm, with Santa photos (get in line by 5:30 pm), Santa's Workshop and a soft play area for toddlers.
- **Brio Park**, 300 S. Euclid St., with a petting zoo and pony rides from 10 am to 6 pm, plus carnival games and a kid roller coaster until 8 pm.
- **The Children's Museum at La Habra**, 301 S. Euclid St., with 45-minute sessions starting every hour from 10 am to 5 pm.

Kids Zone wristbands are $20 if you buy ahead and $25 on the day. You can buy them early at the Children's Museum (Nov. 28 to Dec. 4) or the La Habra Community Center (Nov. 30 to Dec. 4). Some attractions also sell $5 single tickets, and a few things, like face painting and the petting zoo, are free.

**Getting there**

A free shuttle runs from off-site parking about every 15 minutes, from 9 am to 11 pm. Pickup spots are Whittier Christian High School, 501 N. Beach Blvd., and La Bonita Park, 500 Granada Ct. (lower lot only, pickup at Idaho and Fahringer Way). Uber and Lyft drop off at 101 W. La Habra Blvd.

Questions go to the La Habra Community Center at 562-383-4200. The festival is on our [events calendar](/events/).
