The La Habra City Council met Monday night, Nov. 16, and the biggest item on the agenda was a new rule about watering grass that's just for show. The official votes will be added here once the city approves the meeting minutes, which usually takes about two weeks.

**Decorative grass**

Sitting with the city's Utility Authority, the council was set to hold a public hearing and first vote on an ordinance about "nonfunctional turf." That's lawn that's there for looks, like grass in parking lots, street medians and in front of businesses. Grass people actually use, like park fields and play areas, isn't part of it.

A state law passed in 2023, AB 1572, bans using drinking water on that kind of grass and gives cities until Jan. 1, 2027 to put the rule in their own codes. It phases in: city and other public property first in 2027, then commercial, industrial and institutional property in 2028, then HOA and condo common areas in 2029. Watering to keep trees healthy, or for health and safety reasons, would still be allowed. The draft does not cover single-family homes.

If the council moved it forward, the ordinance still needs a second vote at a later meeting before it becomes law.

**Zoning cleanup**

A second public hearing covered Zone Change 26-0003, a batch of small edits to the city's zoning code meant to make the rules more consistent and quicker to apply. The Planning Commission recommended it 3-0 in October. Like the turf rule, it was up for a first reading, so a second vote would come later.

**Sidewalks, striping and EV chargers**

The consent calendar, usually passed in one vote, included:

- A sidewalk grinding program, up to $442,981, and a sidewalk improvement project, up to $585,612
- Annual street striping, up to $191,980
- An EV charging station project at the La Habra Community Center, up to $119,850
- A new full-size riding mower for the city fleet, $197,895
- A $35,374 contract with Pyro Spectaculars for the 2027 Fourth of July fireworks

The council also recognized the La Habra Pop Warner 10U team for winning the 2026 Orange Bowl. The 5:30 pm closed session was canceled.

The next council meeting is Monday, Dec. 7, at 6:30 pm at City Hall, 100 E. La Habra Blvd.
