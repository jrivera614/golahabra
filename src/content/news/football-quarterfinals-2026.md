Two local football teams are still alive in the CIF Southern Section playoffs, and both play quarterfinal games this Friday, Nov. 13.

**La Habra hosts Cathedral (Division 4)**

The Highlanders are home Friday at 7 pm against Cathedral, from Los Angeles.

La Habra got here the hard way. The team finished the regular season at 5-5, then beat Etiwanda 42-35 at home in the first round on Thursday, Nov. 5. That win puts the Highlanders at 6-5. Cathedral reached the quarterfinals by beating Cypress 41-21.

**Whittier Christian at Whittier (Division 13)**

The Heralds play at Whittier on Friday. Kickoff time hasn't been posted yet. CIF-SS playoff games usually start at 7 pm unless the schools set a different time, so check before you leave.

Whittier Christian beat La Sierra 40-36 at home in the first round on Friday, Nov. 6, and is now 8-3.

**Sonora's season is over**

Sonora's run ended in the Division 10 first round with a 31-0 home loss to Desert Hot Springs on Nov. 6. The Raiders finish 9-2 after a 9-1 regular season, the best of our three local teams.
